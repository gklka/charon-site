Privacy Policy

Charon doesn't collect any data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your devices

  • Computers and settings you add are stored on your device.
  • Passwords are stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Screen thumbnails of the computers you connected to, and a diagnostic log, are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded.

Sync

  • If you use iCloud Keychain, your computers, passwords and settings sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through Apple's iCloud Keychain, end-to-end encrypted. The developer has no access to it.
  • When you pair an Apple TV or Apple Watch, the same data is sent directly between your own devices, over your local network or through the Watch connection.

Network connections

Charon connects only to the computers you choose, and looks for computers on your local network with Bonjour. Sending a Wake on LAN packet stays on your local network. There are no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

Changes
