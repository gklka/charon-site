Charon

A remote desktop client for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch. It connects to Macs and other computers over VNC (Screen Sharing), and to Windows and Linux over RDP (Remote Desktop).

Getting started

  • Mac: turn on Screen Sharing in System Settings > General > Sharing. Charon finds the Mac on your network; sign in with a user account on that Mac.
  • Windows: turn on Remote Desktop in Settings > System > Remote Desktop, then add the PC by its name or IP address and sign in with a Windows account.
  • Linux and other computers: any VNC server works, and RDP servers such as GNOME Remote Desktop and xrdp.

Common questions

Charon can't find my computer. Make sure Charon has Local Network access (Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network) and that the computer is on the same network. You can always add a computer by its address.

Can I use an external display? Yes. Connect a monitor to your iPhone or iPad with a USB-C or Lightning adapter, or use AirPlay. The remote computer fills the monitor and your device becomes the trackpad and keyboard.

How do my computers get to Apple TV? In Charon on Apple TV, open Settings and pair it with your iPhone or iPad by scanning the code. Computers and passwords then sync over your local network.

Where are my passwords stored? In your Keychain. iCloud Keychain syncs them between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. See the privacy policy.
